I am a senior studying Computer Science at the University of Houston (GPA 3.73), graduating in December 2026. I work at the intersection of mathematical optimization and machine learning. I build optimization models for energy systems, and I study how to make large language models reliable when they formulate optimization problems.

Publications

Right Answer, Wrong Model: Verifying LLM-Generated Optimization Models

Amr Mohamed · Under review at ICLR 2027

TL;DR: LLMs can write optimization models that report the correct objective value but have wrong constraints. 14.9% of models from 7 LLMs had these silent errors. SilentOR catches them with executable checks and solver-backed witnesses, raising detection precision from 66.4% to 96.2%.

Read the paperCode

Efficiency-Aware Optimization of Wind-Powered Electrolyzer Operation with Multiple Operating States

Amr Mohamed, J. Huang, L. Fan · North American Power Symposium (NAPS), 2026

TL;DR: A full-year hourly MILP for wind-to-hydrogen production. Adding a standby state beside on/off operation raises annual profit by 4.2% and solves faster.
