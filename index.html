I am a Computer Science student at the University of Houston working on mathematical optimization and on how AI can formulate and solve optimization problems reliably.

I build optimization models for energy systems with Prof. Lei Fan and Prof. Nikolaos Tsekos, and I have worked on vision foundation models with Prof. Christoph Eick. I graduate in December 2026 and am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.

Research projects

Interior-Point Methods for Power Grid Optimization

With Prof. Nikolaos Tsekos · Fall 2026 – present

A primal-dual interior-point method for DC optimal power flow on IEEE 30/118-bus grids with Google data-center loads. I compare Cholesky, CG, and Jacobi-PCG solvers and validate against Gurobi.

GitHub

Oyster-Reef Segmentation with SAM 2

With Prof. Christoph Eick · Fall 2025

LoRA-fine-tuned SAM 2, prompted with DINOv2-derived masks, segments oyster reefs in drone imagery at 0.73 IoU.

GitHub

QUBO Optimization for Nuclear Reactor Fuel Loading

NSF RET, with Prof. Lei Fan · Summer 2025

A 37-cell core-loading problem formulated as a QUBO and solved with simulated annealing, reaching zero same-type adjacencies. I turned it into a high-school lesson on matrices.
